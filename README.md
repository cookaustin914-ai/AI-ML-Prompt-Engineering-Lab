# AI-ML-Prompt-Engineering-Lab
The purpose of this lab is to apply prompt engineering techniques to generate a SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis for a company of your choice within your desired field of work by exploring job postings on Indeed.com to select a company, generating SWOT analyses based on said company, and refining those initial prompts to improve later for an inteview context.
The company I have chosen is one that specializes in Information Technology called LTM Limited. LTM Limited is an AI-centric global technology services company based in Mumbai, India.
